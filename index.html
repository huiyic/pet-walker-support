Steps aren’t appearing

Open Settings → Health permissions in the app and connect Apple Health. Check that Pet Walker has permission to read steps in Apple Health, then return to the app to refresh. Health updates may take a little time. If you use an older Motion & Fitness connection, check that permission in iOS Settings too.

Where is my progress saved?

Your pet and progress are saved on this device. The current version has no account or app-provided cloud backup, so progress does not automatically sync to another phone. Please contact us before deleting the app to troubleshoot a problem; deleting it removes local progress.

How do I save a copy of my data?

Open Settings → Privacy → Export my data and choose a destination in the iPhone share sheet. This creates a readable copy of your profile and progress. The app does not currently offer a way to import that file to restore a game.

How do I change reminders?

Open the app’s Settings → Notifications to adjust reminders. You can also manage Pet Walker notifications in iOS Settings.

How do I change analytics sharing?

Open Account → Privacy → Share usage analytics. Sharing is optional and starts only after you choose to enable it. Turning it off stops future analytics collection. For details about PostHog and previously collected events, read our privacy policy.

How do I remove my data?

Delete the app to remove its local data; offloading the app keeps its data. Manage any exported files and device backups separately. Your Apple Health records are managed in Apple Health. Deleting the app does not automatically delete previously shared analytics. For analytics or information you sent by email, contact us to request deletion.

Privacy questions

Read our privacy policy for details about step permissions, local storage, optional usage analytics, and support correspondence. You can send privacy requests to the same support email above.
