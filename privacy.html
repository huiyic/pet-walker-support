Privacy policy

Your walks help Piko grow. Your step counts and saved pet progress stay on your device. If you choose to share usage analytics, selected gameplay actions are sent to PostHog to help us improve Piko.

Last updated: September 29, 2026 · Account-free iOS version

About this policy

This policy explains how Pet Walker, the virtual pet app featuring Piko, handles information. For privacy questions, contact junxiang.lee@tabitalk.com.

Information used on your device

The app stores your chosen display name, pet and room progress, collected items, Energy, walking totals and history, and preferences on your device. This information makes gameplay, walking rewards, accessibility settings, and reminders work. The current version does not require an account and does not upload your saved profile, step history, or full game save to a cloud-save service. Optional usage analytics, described below, records selected actions separately from your saved progress.

Apple Health and step counts

With your permission, Pet Walker reads step counts from Apple Health to turn walking into in-game Energy and show walking history. It can read recent daily totals to catch up when you return. Existing Motion & Fitness connections may continue using the iPhone’s step counter until reconnected to Apple Health.

The app requests read-only access to step counts. It does not write to Apple Health, read other Health data types, or access your location, contacts, or photos. Health and motion data are not sold, used for advertising, or sent to analytics services.

You can stop future step reading in the app’s Health permissions settings. You can also revoke step access in Apple Health or the relevant iOS privacy settings. Stopping access does not erase totals already saved in the app or change your original Apple Health records.

Optional usage analytics

In versions with analytics enabled, we ask whether you want to share usage analytics after the introductory comic, when you reach Home. If you choose Share usage analytics, we use PostHog to understand which features players use, where onboarding is confusing, and whether players return. Choosing Play without sharing leaves analytics off and does not affect gameplay.

Analytics includes app opens, screens visited, button taps, onboarding steps completed after consent, and successful gameplay actions such as feeding Piko, playing, exploring, and equipping or buying room items with in-game Energy. Events may include the relevant screen, button, item, room slot, or exploration type, along with timestamps, app/version information, and technical device information such as operating system and device model.

PostHog assigns a random identifier to the app installation and uses session identifiers to group events. These identifiers let us recognize activity from the same installation without receiving your name or email. They are not advertising identifiers, and we do not link them to an account. We do not send your display name, email, step counts, walking history, Health data, Energy balance, or full saved game to PostHog.

PostHog processes this analytics data for us using our US Cloud project, hosted in the United States. As with an internet connection, the receiving service can process your IP address to receive and operate the request. Our app events request that IP-based location enrichment be disabled. See PostHog’s privacy policy for information about the provider.

You can turn sharing on or off under Account → Privacy → Share usage analytics. Turning it off stops future analytics collection; it does not automatically erase events already received. Session replay and screen recording are disabled in the current release configuration.

Notifications and data export

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders and exploration updates locally on your device. You can change these preferences in the app or disable notifications in iOS Settings.

When you choose Settings → Privacy → Export my data, the app creates a copy of your profile and game progress and opens the iPhone share sheet. The exported copy is sent to the destination you choose; that destination handles it under its own privacy practices. This export contains profile and game progress, not the analytics history held by PostHog.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include, such as a message or screenshot. We use this information to respond to your request and troubleshoot issues. Our email service processes this correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive health information that is not needed to explain the problem.

Sharing, advertising, and this website

The current app does not include advertising or cross-app advertising tracking. Optional gameplay analytics is processed by PostHog as described above. We do not sell your personal information. Support correspondence is used for support and may be disclosed when required by law.

These pages do not use analytics scripts, advertising, or cookies set by us. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical information about visits, including IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Your email provider and Apple’s services have their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until the app’s data is removed. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data. Pet Walker does not offer its own cloud backup in this version, and we cannot restore deleted local progress. Copies in device backups or exports you create are controlled by you and the relevant backup or storage service. Deleting Pet Walker does not delete your original Apple Health data.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and any related legal obligations. You may email us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us. We cannot remotely access or erase game data stored only on your device.

Previously collected analytics is stored in our PostHog project under its applicable retention settings. Turning off analytics or deleting the app does not automatically delete those stored events. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of analytics associated with your installation. Because we do not attach your name or email to analytics, we may need additional information to locate the relevant records and may not be able to identify them from an email address alone.

Your choices and questions

You can choose whether to share usage analytics, grant health and notification permissions, edit your display name, export your data, or remove local app data. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. To make a request or raise a concern, email junxiang.lee@tabitalk.com.

Policy updates

We will update this page when the app’s privacy practices change and revise the date above. This policy covers the account-free version; any future account or cloud-save features will need an updated policy describing their data handling.
